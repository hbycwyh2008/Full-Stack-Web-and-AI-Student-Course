# AI Use Policy for Students

AI is allowed as a **learning tool**, not a **shortcut**.

![AI Use Policy](../public-documents/posters/ai-use-policy.png)

Full policy: [ai-usage-policy.md](../assessment/ai-usage-policy.md)  
During class: [ai-use-during-practice](../class-missions/shared/ai-use-during-practice.md)

---

## You May Use AI To

- Explain concepts you do not understand yet
- Debug errors (after you tried yourself)
- Get hints — not full solutions
- Review your work and suggest improvements
- Practice explaining ideas

---

## You May Not Use AI To

- Complete entire tasks or projects for you
- Submit work you cannot **explain** orally
- Skip **Independent Rebuild** in class
- Copy code without reading, testing, or revising
- Hide AI help — you must disclose it

---

## You Must

1. **Understand** what AI suggested
2. **Modify and test** the code yourself
3. **Debug** when something breaks
4. Write an **AI usage note** when AI helped

Short form:

```text
AI Help Used: Yes / No
What I asked AI:
How it helped me:
What I did on my own:
```

Full template: [ai-usage-reflection-template.md](../templates/ai-usage-reflection-template.md)

---

## Why This Matters

**Mastery** means you can **explain, rebuild, debug, modify, and apply** — not paste AI output.

If you cannot explain your project without reading AI text, the evidence is incomplete.

---

## In Class

During **Guided Practice** and **Independent Rebuild**, follow your teacher’s rules. When unsure, ask before using AI.
