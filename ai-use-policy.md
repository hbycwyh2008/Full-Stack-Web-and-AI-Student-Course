# AI Use Policy

AI is allowed as a learning tool. AI is not allowed as a replacement for your understanding.

## You May Use AI To

- Explain a concept in simpler words
- Give hints
- Help read an error message
- Suggest debugging steps
- Review your code
- Help you practice explaining your work

## You May Not Use AI To

- Submit work you cannot explain
- Hide AI usage
- Copy a full solution and call it your own
- Skip the independent rebuild
- Avoid debugging or reflection

## Required AI Usage Note

If AI helped, include this note in your evidence:

```text
AI Help Used:
What I asked AI:
How it helped me:
What I did on my own:
```

If you used no AI, write `No AI used` if your teacher asks for a note.
